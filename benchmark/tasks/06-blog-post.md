Add a blog post to my website called "Why we moved to a four-day week", dated 2 November 2026, and make sure it shows up on the blog page. Here's the text:

Six months ago we switched the whole team to a four-day week. We expected a dip in output. It never came.

Fewer days made us protective of our time. Meetings got shorter, and most of them turned into written updates that people read when it suited them.

We're not going back. If you're thinking about trying it, start with a three-month trial and measure what matters to your customers.
